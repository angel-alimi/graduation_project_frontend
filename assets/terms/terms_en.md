# LifeShare Terms and Conditions

Last updated: November 9, 2026

## 1. Acceptance of These Terms

By creating an account or using LifeShare, you agree to these Terms and Conditions and to how we handle your data as described in Section 9.

LifeShare is a platform that connects blood donors with people who need blood for themselves or for someone else. The app is a communication and coordination tool only. It does not collect, store, test, transport, or supply blood.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| User | Any person with a LifeShare account, acting as a donor, a requester, or both |
| Donor | A user who responds to a blood request |
| Requester | A user who creates a blood request for themselves or for another person |
| LifeShare team | The people who develop and run the app |
| Request | A blood request published on the app |
| Eligibility Questionnaire | The short health form a donor answers before a response is accepted |

## 3. Who Can Use LifeShare and Account Registration

1. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. Donors must also meet the age limits set by the hospital or blood bank where they donate.
2. You register with a valid mobile number verified by a one-time code (OTP). The number must be registered in your name.
3. Each person may hold only one account. Duplicate accounts with the same phone number or email will be rejected or removed.
4. You are responsible for keeping your account secure and for all activity on it. Do not share your OTP or password with anyone, and tell us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.
5. Accounts are for individuals only. You may not create an account in the name of, or impersonate, another person, a hospital, or an organization.

## 4. Medical Disclaimer

LifeShare is not a medical service and does not give medical advice.

- **The questionnaire is a pre-screening only.** Passing the Eligibility Questionnaire does not mean you are cleared to donate. The final decision is always made by qualified staff at the hospital or blood bank, after their own examination and tests.
- **Answer honestly.** Hiding a condition, medication, or recent donation can put the patient who receives your blood at serious risk. You are responsible for the accuracy of your answers.
- **Blood is not given directly.** All donations happen at a hospital or blood bank, where the blood is tested and processed. No user may arrange a direct transfer of blood outside an authorized facility.
- **Talk to a doctor.** If you are unsure whether you can donate because of a health condition, pregnancy, or medication, consult a doctor or the donation unit before going.
- **After donation.** Follow the instructions of the hospital staff. LifeShare is not responsible for any reaction or complication that occurs during or after a donation.

## 5. Your Responsibilities as a User

- Provide accurate, current information, including your name, blood type, date of birth, donation areas, and last donation date.
- Update your profile when your information changes, especially your last donation date if you donated outside LifeShare.
- Only accept a request if you genuinely intend to go and donate. If you can no longer go, tell the requester through the chat as soon as possible so they can find another donor.
- Do not accept the same request more than once. Each accepted response counts as one unit.
- The app stops sending you requests for 56 days after a recorded donation. Hospitals may require a longer waiting period, and their rule always applies.

## 6. Blood Requests and Public Posts

1. **Requests must be real.** Create a request only for a genuine, current medical need at a real hospital. Fake, test, or exaggerated requests are prohibited and lead to account suspension.
2. **Requests are public.** Every request appears on the public feed. Share only the information needed: the patient's first name or initials, blood type, hospital, units needed, and a contact method. Do not post medical reports or ID numbers of the patient.
3. **Get consent.** If you request blood for someone else, you confirm that the patient or their family agrees to publish the request.
4. **Close requests that are no longer needed.** The app closes a request automatically when all its units are confirmed or when the donation or surgery date has passed. If the need ends earlier, close it yourself.
5. **No buying or selling blood.** Blood donation on LifeShare is voluntary and unpaid. Offering, asking for, or accepting money, gifts, or any benefit in exchange for blood is strictly prohibited, results in a permanent ban, and may be reported to the authorities.
6. We may hide or remove any request that breaks these Terms, without prior notice.

## 7. Confirming a Donation

- After donating at the hospital or blood bank, the donor marks the donation as done in the app, and the requester confirms that the donor came and donated.
- A confirmed donation updates the donor's last donation date, starts the 56-day waiting period, and adds reward points.
- Confirm only donations that actually happened. Confirming a fake donation, for example to gain points, leads to account suspension.

## 8. Notifications, SMS, and Location

- By registering, you agree to receive notifications about blood requests that match you and donation reminders (about every 3 months), and SMS messages to verify your phone number.
- You receive at most 3 notifications per day.
- You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings, but you may then miss requests that match you.
- LifeShare uses your live location to match you with donation requests at hospitals near you.
- Delivery of notifications and SMS depends on third parties (Google Firebase and mobile networks) and on your device settings. We cannot guarantee that every message arrives on time.

## 9. Privacy and Health Data

We collect only what is needed to match donors with requests, and you consent to this processing when you register.

| Data | Why we use it | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name, phone number, email | Account, verification, contact about a donation | You; the requester only after you accept their request |
| Blood type, date of birth, donation areas | Matching you with compatible, nearby requests | System only; your blood type is shown to the requester after you accept |
| Location | Matching you with requests at nearby hospitals | System only |
| Last donation date, donation history, reward points | Enforcing the 56-day interval and showing your history | You only |
| Eligibility questionnaire answers | Pre-screening for a specific request | You only; the requester sees only whether you passed |
| Chat messages | Coordinating donations | Chat participants; the LifeShare team only when handling a complaint |

- Your health answers are never shown on the public feed or to other donors.
- We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
- Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored encrypted, and only the LifeShare team can access the database.
- We share data with service providers only as needed to run the app (Google Firebase for notifications, Google Maps for distances, and an SMS provider), and with official authorities only when required by law.
- You can view and correct your data at any time in your profile, and ask us to delete your account (Section 13). After deletion, we keep only anonymous statistics, such as the number of donations made through the app.

## 10. Reward Points

Points are a symbol of appreciation only: they have no cash value, cannot be sold or transferred, and are not payment for blood. We may change or end the points program at any time, and remove points earned through false or fraudulent records.
